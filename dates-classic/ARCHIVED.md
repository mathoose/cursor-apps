# Archived app

**Dates** (the activities filing cabinet + happy-hour list) is hidden from the home screen launcher. Its slot is now **[Local Events](../things-to-do/)**, which lives in `things-to-do/`.

- Saved activities stay in localStorage under `things-to-do-v1`, and the unified backup still exports them under the `things-to-do` entry.
- Open it directly at `/cursor-apps/dates-classic/`, or from the link at the bottom of Local Events.
- Seed activities: `dates-classic/events.json`.
