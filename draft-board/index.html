Your snake picks

Do

  • 1.01: Jahmyr Gibbs. Nick’s official 1.01. In a 2-WR league he wants hero RB, not Chase/Puka first. Bijan is the only other 1.01-quality back.
  • 28 & 29: double-tap WR. That’s your 2–3 turn. Nabers, AJ Brown, Nico, Olave, DeVonta, Pickens, Rice, Tet. Hitting a WR1 here beats the leftover RBs (Jacobs / Kyren / Javonte).

  • Wait on elite TE. Skip Bowers/McBride at 28–29. Warren or Loveland at 56–57; LaPorta / Kraft at 84–85; Chig as a punt around 140.

  • Handcuffs with a locked job: Tank Bigsby, MarShawn Lloyd, Jonathan Brooks (if Chuba is out). 49ers RB2 (Black / James) is free if you drafted CMC.

Don’t

  • Don’t take WR at 1.01 in this format. You’ll be RB-starved by pick 28.
  • Don’t take Jacobs as your RB1 over a WR1 at 28–29 (camp, OL, possible suspension).
  • Don’t draft Kamara, Tracy, Hutchinson, Tank Dell, Ja'Kobi Lane, Trey Harris, Gadsden, Cyrus Allen, or anyone behind Skattebo (Tracy / Singletary / Najee).
  • Don’t pay ADP for Achane, Kyren, Zay Flowers, Garrett Wilson, Alec Pierce, McLaurin, or Jordyn Tyson.
  • Don’t reach Jeremiyah Love if the high ankle is still lingering — he slid from 2–3 turn to 3–4.
  • Don’t smash Mike Washington as a Jeanty cuff — they don’t want that Raiders offense without Jeanty.
  • K / DST last two picks (196–197). Never earlier.
